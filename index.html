My process

From idea to live website in six clear steps

A simple process, so you always know what happens next.

  1. Step 1

    Discover

    We talk through your goals, your audience and what the website needs to achieve.

  2. Step 2

    Plan

    I define the pages, structure and tools before any code is written.

  3. Step 3

    Design

    I shape the layout, colours and typography so the whole site feels consistent.

  4. Step 4

    Develop

    I build the pages with clean, accessible HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

  5. Step 5

    Test

    I check phones, tablets, browsers, speed and accessibility before anything goes live.

  6. Step 6

    Launch

    I publish the site on Netlify, verify every link and hand it over to you.
